## 2024-05-24 - Actionable Empty States & Tab A11y
**Learning:** Found that the main editor empty state was a dead-end text message, and the tab close buttons (`✕`) lacked accessible names and keyboard focus visibility, which are essential for a good desktop app experience.
**Action:** Always provide actionable clear CTAs in empty states and ensure icon-only control buttons include `aria-label`, `title`, and `focus-visible` styles for comprehensive a11y support.
